Privacy Policy - Diazen

Effective Date: 10/05/2025

Your privacy is very important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled within the Diazen application.

1. Information collection

Diazen does not collect personally identifiable information. The app does not request your name, email address, or any contact data. However, by including ads through Google AdMob, third parties may automatically collect certain device information, such as:

  • Advertising ID.
  • Ad interactions (clicks, views).
  • Technical data (device model, operating system, etc.).

This data collection is managed by Google under its own privacy policies.

2. Advertising and third parties

We use Google AdMob to display ads in the app. Google may use technologies such as cookies and SDKs to personalize ads and improve performance. For more details, please review Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You can limit or disable ad personalization from your device settings.

3. Local storage

All information you enter in the app (such as menus or ingredients) is stored only on your device. Diazen does not upload or transmit this data to external servers.

4. Internet connection

The app connects to the internet only to display Google AdMob ads and to check for available updates. No personal data is transmitted.

5. Security

Since no personal data is directly collected, we recommend not sharing your device if you wish to keep your local data private.

6. Children

Diazen is designed for all audiences, including minors. Adult supervision is recommended when a child uses the app, especially due to the presence of third-party advertising.

7. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally. If there are significant changes, we will inform you through the app or official channels. We recommend reviewing it periodically.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer at: wolkar.rblx@gmail.com
